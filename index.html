What We're Building

Replacing the assumptions that make AI expensive

Constant-cost inference

Current AI models get more expensive the longer you talk to them. We've built an architecture where every token costs the same, first or millionth.

Memory that persists

Today's AI forgets everything when you close a chat. Our architecture saves its state to disk and picks up exactly where it left off, no re-reading, no re-computing.

Intelligence at the edge

Our models are designed to run on devices with limited power like phones, robots, and vehicles, not just massive data centers.

A new kind of attention

We replace global self-attention, the bottleneck behind today's AI costs, with five constant-cost mechanisms coordinated by a single learned signal.
